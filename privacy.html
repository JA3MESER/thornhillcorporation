Privacy Notice

This notice explains what personal data Thornhill Corporation collects, why we collect it, how we keep it safe, how long we keep it and what your rights are under UK data protection law.

The short version

  • We only collect what you type into our forms or send us by email, such as your name, email address and message.
  • We only use it to reply to you. We never sell it, never use it for advertising and never use it to build a profile of you.
  • It's sent over an encrypted connection and stored in an encrypted database that the public can't read. Only authorised Thornhill staff can see it.
  • We delete it automatically, 12 months after your enquiry is resolved (24 months at the most).
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time, free of charge.
  • We're based in the United Kingdom and only provide our services to people who live in the UK.
  • Never send us your passwords, Roblox 2-Step Verification codes or bank details. We'll never ask for them.

1. Who we are

Thornhill Corporation ("we", "us") is based in the United Kingdom. We are the controller of the personal data described in this notice, which means we decide how and why it's used and we're responsible for protecting it.

You can contact us about anything in this notice at support@thornhill.example. Please put "Privacy" in the subject line.

2. What we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us:

WhereWhat we collect
Support formYour full name, email address, enquiry type, subject and message, and your confirmation that you live in the UK, are 13 or over (or are a parent or guardian) and have read this notice.
Roblox Advice formYour full name, email address, subject and details of your request, the priority you choose, which part of the UK you live in, any extra information you add, and the confirmations you tick.

We don't ask for your address, date of birth, phone number or payment details, and we don't collect information about you from other places. We don't use analytics, tracking or advertising tools.

Please don't include sensitive information (for example about your health) in a form or email unless it's needed to answer your question.

3. How we use it and why

UK data protection law says we must have a valid reason (a "lawful basis") for using your personal data. These are ours:

What we doLawful basis
Read and reply to your enquiry or Roblox Advice request, and send an automatic email confirming we've received itOur legitimate interests in answering people who contact us, and taking steps you've asked us to take
Keep a record of our conversation so we can follow up and stay consistentLegitimate interests
Keep the website and database secure, stop spam and prevent misuseLegitimate interests
Keep information if the law requires us to, or to deal with a complaint or claimLegal obligation or legitimate interests

We won't send you marketing. We don't make decisions about you using automated tools, and we don't profile you.

4. How we keep it secure

We take the security of your information seriously and use several layers of protection:

  • Encrypted in transit. Everything you send through this website travels over an encrypted HTTPS connection.
  • Encrypted at rest. Our database provider, Supabase, encrypts stored data with AES-256.
  • Closed to the public. Every table in our database is locked with row-level security. The website can only add a new enquiry. It can't read, change or list anyone's information.
  • Staff-only access. Only authorised Thornhill staff can see enquiries. Each person has their own sign-in, and access codes are stored only as scrambled hashes, never as plain text.
  • Strong sign-in protection. Staff can use passkeys (fingerprint, face or device PIN) through Thornhill Quick. Accounts lock after 5 wrong attempts, and sign-ins expire automatically.
  • Spam and abuse limits. We limit how many forms can be sent in a short time.
  • Less data, kept for less time. We only ask for what we need, and old records are deleted automatically (see section 6).

No website or database can ever be 100% secure, but we'll keep reviewing these measures. If a data breach is likely to put you at risk, we'll tell you and, where the law requires it, report it to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) within 72 hours.

We never sell or rent your personal data. We only share it with the trusted services that run our website and email. They act on our instructions under data processing agreements:

We may also share information if the law requires us to, for example with the police or a court.

7. Your data rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • See your data. Ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct it. Ask us to fix anything that's wrong or incomplete.
  • Delete it. Ask us to erase your data.
  • Restrict it. Ask us to pause using your data.
  • Object. Object to us using your data based on our legitimate interests.
  • Move it. Ask for your data in a format you can take elsewhere.

To use any of these rights, email support@thornhill.example with "Privacy" in the subject. It's free, and we'll reply within one month. We may need to confirm who you are first, so we don't give your information to someone else.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator, at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

8. Children and young people

Lots of Roblox players are young, so we've designed our forms with children's privacy in mind. We ask for as little information as possible, we have no tracking or advertising, and nothing is shared publicly.

Our forms are for people aged 13 or over. If you're under 13, please ask a parent or guardian to fill in the form for you. If you're under 18, it's a good idea to tell a parent, guardian or trusted adult before you contact us.

If a parent or guardian thinks their child has sent us information, they can ask us to delete it using the contact details above.
