About us

A new corporation with a clear mission.

Thornhill Corporation was established in 2026. Our mission is to strive for success in marketing, media, development and finance — four areas that work best when they work together.

We also offer legal aid to our clients. Whatever we're working on, we always have their best interests at heart.

  • Four disciplines under one roof: marketing, media, development and finance
  • Legal aid for clients when they need it
  • Every decision made with our clients' best interests at heart
