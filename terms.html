Thornhill Corporation operates from the United Kingdom, and our support and Roblox Advice services are only available to people who live in the United Kingdom (England, Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland). 3. Roblox Advice is general guidance only

Roblox Advice requests are reviewed by a Roblox Developer, who will share general guidance based on their experience of building and running Roblox games, groups and communities.

  • Our advice is not legal, financial or professional advice. Our team are not solicitors, lawyers or regulated financial advisers, and we can't act for you in any dispute.
  • We can't guarantee any particular result. You decide what to do with our guidance, and you should check anything important for yourself.
  • If you need legal help, contact a qualified solicitor or Citizens Advice. For problems with a Roblox account, purchase or moderation decision, contact Roblox Support.
  • We may decline any request, for example if it's outside what we can help with, breaks these terms or breaks Roblox's rules.

Our Development and Media services are general guidance too. They don't include regulated legal, financial or investment advice, and you decide what to buy or post.
